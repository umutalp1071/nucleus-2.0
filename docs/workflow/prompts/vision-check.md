# NORTH STAR VISION

Nucleus 2.0 exists to enable:

> "Everyone should have their own AI venture studio — running on their own computer, under their own control, without requiring deep technical expertise, with an affordable budget."

The goal is not just building software.

The goal is creating an AI-native system that helps people build, operate, and improve products.

# EVALUATION CRITERIA

Review recent work against these questions:

## 1. Vision Alignment

Does this work move us closer to:

- Making AI venture creation easier?
- Reducing unnecessary technical barriers?
- Giving users more capability and independence?

---

## 2. User Value

Ask:

- Does this improve the user's ability to create, launch, or operate products?
- Are we solving a meaningful problem?

---

## 3. AI-Native Principle

Ask:

- Are we building a system where AI is a core capability, not just an added feature?
- Does this follow the Nucleus 2.0 architecture philosophy?

---

## 4. Complexity Check

Ask:

- Are we removing complexity for users?
- Are we accidentally exposing internal complexity?
- Are we building infrastructure that supports the vision, or just adding features?

---

## 5. Scope Check

Ask:

- Are we advancing the AI Venture Studio vision?
- Or are we drifting into a small standalone SaaS/product?
